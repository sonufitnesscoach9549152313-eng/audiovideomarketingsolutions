      About

      25+ Years of Turning Ideas Into Impact

      Audio Video Marketing Solution began with a small audio-recording setup near the local "Alok CD" gali in Sikar, at a time when CDs were the main way audio was recorded and shared.

      Over 25+ years, that work grew from recording voices onto CDs into a full audio, video, advertising and digital marketing company serving local businesses, institutions and communities.
